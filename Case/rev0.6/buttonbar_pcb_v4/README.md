# Button bar PCB v4 (case rev 0.6) - 2026-11-15

One 2-layer board (176.7 x 11.5 mm, 1.6 mm) with everything on it: no hand wiring.
Open `buttonbar_v4.kicad_pro` in KiCad 7-10 (footprints in `buttonbar_v4.pretty`). Gerbers + drill for JLCPCB:
`buttonbar_v4_gerbers.zip`. Status: fully routed, 0 unconnected, 0 copper/clearance errors (only silkscreen
cosmetics in `drc.rpt`). Generated by `scripts/run.sh` (placement in `design.py`, Freerouting).
No schematic sheet yet: the netlist lives in `design.py`.

## What is on it

| Ref | Part | Side | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| SW1-9 | TC-0120 slim tactile (Glenn's, long plunger) | front | slotted holes 1.9 x 0.9, legs 7.4 apart (6.8-8.0 fits) |
| D1-D10 | SS14 Schottky, SMA | back | stripe (cathode) toward the switch |
| U1 | szfydosh BH1750 module, castellated | front | soldered flat, component side up; ADDR tied to GND (0x23) |
| J1 | JST B5B-PH-K-S (vertical) | back | to main board **J12** pin for pin: 1 screen, 2 vol+, 3 vol-, 4 mute, 5 GND |
| J2 | JST B4B-PH-K-S (vertical) | back | to main board **J7** pin for pin: 1 3V3, 2 GND, 3 SCL, 4 SDA |
| H1-H3 | M3 holes 3.4 | | M3x12 countersunk from the back into M3 inserts in bar_front |
| H4-H5 | 6.6 holes | | the clamp-plate screw ears reach through here (M3x6 countersunk from behind) |

Cables: two straight PH cables (PHR-5 to PHR-5, PHR-4 to PHR-4), pin 1 to pin 1.

Diode coding (unchanged): single buttons pull one J12 line low; combos pull two lines low through SS14s.
Home S+D, Prev D+M, Play U+D, Next U+M, Voice S+U. Firmware decodes the pattern after ~30 ms.

## Check before ordering
- BH1750 pads: 2.54 mm pitch (Glenn confirmed 2026-11-15) along the 11.5 mm edges, centred, castellation centres on the board edge (from the
  datasheet + photos, not measured with calipers). Seen from the component side, left column top to bottom
  NC, GND, ADDR, 3V3; right column SDA, SCL, GND, VCC.
- Board is longer than 100 mm, so it is not the $2 JLCPCB promo: upload the zip at jlcpcb.com/quote for the price.

## Case parts (case/stl/v4_draft, model car_radio_rev06_case.scad with pcb4_data.scad)
Only M3 screws (Glenn 2026-11-15), all countersunk (DIN 7991):

| Where | Screw | Qty | Into |
|---|---|---|---|
| bar_back + PCB -> bar_front | M3x12 | 3 | M3 heat inserts in bar_front (pressed in from the PCB side) |
| cap_plate -> bar_front | M3x6, NOT longer (M3x8 comes through the front) | 2 | straight into the printed pillars (2.5 pilot) |
| bar tongue -> screen tray | M3x6 | 4 | M3 short inserts in the tray: on the printed tray drill the 4 M2 insert holes to 4.0 |

bar_front: M3 bosses 7.6 mm. bar_back: 2.5 mm base, 2 full-length ribs + cross ribs. cap_plate holds the loose caps
(current v3 caps).
